// How I Work

We Build Custom AI Agents & Applications for Your Business

Think of it like your own intelligent assistant that knows all your data, builds what you need, and works exactly how you want—no matter your industry or workflow.

Custom AI Agent Connected to Your Data

We build an AI agent trained on YOUR data that answers questions, generates reports, and gives insights in plain English—like having a business analyst who knows everything about your company.

  • Your AI knows your business—trained on Salesforce, QuickBooks, emails, contracts, past projects (unified in Snowflake, BigQuery, or PostgreSQL)
  • Ask anything, get instant answers—"What were our Q3 margins?" or "Find all contracts with Company X" using RAG and vector search (Pinecone, Weaviate)
  • Custom dashboards built for you—track your specific KPIs and metrics (Tableau, Power BI, or fully bespoke)

Bespoke AI Applications Built for Your Workflow

We build custom AI-powered applications tailored to your exact needs—not off-the-shelf software. Your own tools that work the way you work.

  • Built for YOUR process—custom web and mobile apps that match your workflow, not generic software you have to adapt to
  • AI that understands your industry—trained on your products, processes, and terminology (Architecture? Healthcare? We speak your language)
  • Examples: Project estimators, client portals, document processors, scheduling systems—whatever your business needs

Intelligent Automation That Scales

Your AI agent doesn't just answer questions—it takes action. Automate repetitive tasks, generate reports, route customers, and handle the work that's slowing you down.

  • AI that does the work—automatically generate reports, process documents, enter data, and handle routine tasks so your team focuses on high-value work
  • Seamless integration—your AI agent connects to existing systems (CRM, ERP, email, databases) and orchestrates workflows across all of them
  • Production-ready and scalable—built with React, Next.js, Python, Swift for reliability as your business grows

// Background

About

I'm a strategist and builder who helps businesses turn their data into competitive advantage. Over the past few years, I've led strategic initiatives at IQVIA, Google, and UnitedHealthcare—working with companies to understand their operations, build BI infrastructure, and deploy AI trained on their specific data.

The pattern I see repeatedly: companies have valuable data scattered across systems (Salesforce, QuickBooks, spreadsheets, email), manual processes eating up hours every week, and no way to ask intelligent questions of their data. They know there's value there, but can't access it.

That's where I come in. I take a strategic approach: first understand your business and bottlenecks, then set up the BI tools to collect your data, train bespoke AI on your specific information, and build automation to streamline your operations. I've done this with my own apps (UX Research Copilot, AEC Hub, Reno AI) and now I'm doing it for businesses.

I'm currently available for consulting projects. If you have data in multiple places, manual processes wasting time and money, or you want AI that actually understands YOUR business—let's talk about building a strategic solution.
