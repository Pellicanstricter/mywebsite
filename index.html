// About

About

I'm a UX researcher who builds end-to-end.

Most researchers hand off findings and hope something ships. I lead the research -- 50+ studies across healthcare, enterprise software, and design technology -- then I build. Full-stack applications, AI agents, data pipelines, production features. Not prototypes-to-prove-a-point. Working software that solves the problem the research identified.

I got here through an unusual path. I started in architecture -- designing buildings, managing construction projects in Central America, teaching computational design. That background gave me a bias toward making things, not just analyzing them. When I moved into product research, I brought the builder's instinct with me.

The tools I work with reflect that range: Python, React, Next.js on the engineering side. FastAPI, GPT-4, Claude, FAISS, CoreML on the AI side. Dovetail, Maze, Mixpanel, and good old spreadsheets on the research side. I don't think of these as separate skill sets. They're how I close the gap between insight and implementation.

Based in Atlanta. Currently at IQVIA. I do my best work on teams where research and engineering aren't separate conversations.
