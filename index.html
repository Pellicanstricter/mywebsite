About

I help businesses turn scattered data into working AI. I've led projects at IQVIA, Google, and UnitedHealthcare—building BI systems and deploying AI trained on real business data.

The pattern I see: companies have data everywhere (Salesforce, QuickBooks, spreadsheets, email) but no way to ask it questions or automate with it. Manual processes waste hours every week.

I fix that. Understand your business, connect your data, train AI on it, build what you need. I've done it with my own apps (UX Research Copilot, AEC Hub, Reno AI). Now I do it for businesses.

Currently available for projects. If you have data in multiple places or manual processes wasting time—let's talk.
