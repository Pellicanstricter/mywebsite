📄 Printing Instructions

Standard Business Card Size: 3.5" × 2" (89mm × 51mm)

How to print:

  • Print at 300 DPI for best quality
  • Use heavy cardstock (14pt or 16pt recommended)
  • Single-sided design (no back needed)
  • Consider professional printing services like Vistaprint, Moo, or local print shops
  • Add 1/8" bleed if using professional printing

Digital Use: Right-click on the card to "Save Image As" for sharing digitally or use as email signature
